Segment playbook

  • At Risk (22.6k): win-back email + 15% coupon

What the numbers say: this is a one-and-done marketplace - only 3.0% of buyers ever order twice, so retention is the biggest untapped lever. Value is concentrated: 23.8% of customers (At Risk) hold 43% of revenue but have not ordered in 6+ months. A win-back campaign aimed at those 22,563 lapsed high-value buyers is the cheapest revenue available.
