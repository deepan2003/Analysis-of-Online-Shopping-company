What the numbers say: revenue rose from R$ 7.09M in 2017 to R$ 8.59M in the first 8 months of 2018 - a 21% increase. Growth comes from order volume, not basket size: AOV stayed between R$ 148 and R$ 170 while monthly orders grew from 787 to 7,187. Nov 2017 is the clear peak (R$ 1.17M, Black Friday); the December drop is a normal post-peak correction, not a demand problem.
