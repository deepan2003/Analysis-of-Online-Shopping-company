What the numbers say: the network is 91.9% on time and delivers 12 days early on average, yet 7,826 orders arrived late - and when late they are late by 8.9 days. Geography drives the risk: SP delivers in 8.7 days with 94.1% on-time, while AL, MA and PI take 19-25 days and miss 16-24% of promises. The North-East corridor is where a logistics fix pays back fastest.
